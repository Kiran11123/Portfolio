01 · About

Where business questions turn into data answers.

I work as a business analyst inside digital transformation projects, which in practice means sitting between the people who need an answer and the data that can give it to them. Three-plus years in, the job has stayed the same in shape even as the tools and industries have changed: gather requirements properly, define KPIs that stakeholders actually agree on, then build and automate the reporting or process behind them.

Most recently at Danone, that meant rebuilding co-manufacturer performance reporting, cutting manual processing time by roughly 90% and removing the errors that came with it. At TCS, it meant automating a core piece of the job development process with a Python script, bringing delivery time down by 85%. In between, I have worked on Power BI and Jira integrations, prototyping, and product research, always circling back to the same question: what does this data need to prove, and to whom.
